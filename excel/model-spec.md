# Advanced Excel Decision Model

## Inputs
- Price increase %
- Discount rate
- Unit cost inflation
- Customer retention rate
- Product mix

## Outputs
- Revenue
- COGS
- Gross profit
- Gross margin
- Leakage recovered
- Annualized EBITDA impact

## Scenarios
1. Current state
2. Reduce average discount by 2 pp
3. Increase prices by 3%
4. Combined pricing + discount intervention

Use Power Query for ingestion, XLOOKUP/SUMIFS for reconciliation, PivotTables for diagnostics, Data Tables for sensitivity analysis, and a management summary sheet for decision output.
