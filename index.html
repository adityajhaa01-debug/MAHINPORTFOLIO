Management Readout

Decision-oriented findings from the analysis layer.

1. Discounting is the primary controllable leak. Average discount rose from 11.2% to 17.0%, accounting for ₹3.1 Cr of modeled annual leakage.
2. Revenue growth is concentrated in low-margin accounts. The top 20 customers contribute 41% of revenue but only 27% of contribution profit.
3. Contract-price variance is creating silent leakage. 7.4% of sampled invoices fall below the contracted floor price, concentrated in three sales territories.
